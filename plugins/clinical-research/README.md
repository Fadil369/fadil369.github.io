# Clinical research

The `research-planning` skill drafts research questions, study design considerations and open questions from public or entirely fictional material. It does not collect participants, access records, conduct a trial or replace research ethics review.

Do not enter patient or participant data. Skill instructions are not an enforceable privacy or clinical-safety boundary; outputs require qualified human review and organizational approval.

Use the [marketplace setup and safety guidance](../../docs/CLINICAL_PLUGIN_MARKETPLACE.md). After the marketplace is published, install this plugin with:

```sh
copilot plugin install clinical-research@brainsait-clinical
```

Local test: `copilot --plugin-dir ./plugins/clinical-research`. Example: “Use research-planning to outline a fictional study question about clinic appointment reminders.”
